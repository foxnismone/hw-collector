This article is a '''list of [[Hot Wheels]]''' released in '''1979.'''

[[Image:1979 Patches.jpg|300px]]


* All of the Vehicles were made in Hong Kong
* All of the Vehicles have Basic/Black Wall wheels [[BW]]
* Each car was advertised with a Free Patch with the proof of 6 Purchases. Each Patch was based on all the sub-lines within the 1979 line.
