This is a list of [[Treasure Hunts]] from 2026.

==2026 Treasure Hunts==
The 2026 Treasure Hunts Series is part of the [[List of 2026 Hot Wheels|2026 Hot Wheels line]].

Since 2013, Mattel has incorporated Treasure Hunts into other series instead of being their own series.

Treasure Hunts are distinguished by the 'circle flame' symbol. [[File:RTH.png|30px]]

==2026 Super Treasure Hunts==
In 2026, Mattel released a series of Super Treasure Hunts, hidden among the other series.

They are distinguished by the following: Spectraflame paint, Real Riders and a [[File:STH.png|30px]] symbol.

A new feature that was added in 2015 was a flame within a circle behind the vehicle on the card, similar to Treasure Hunts, but colored in gold.
