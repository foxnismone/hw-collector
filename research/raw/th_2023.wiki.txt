
==2023 Treasure Hunts==
The 2023 Treasure Hunts Series is part of the [[List of 2023 Hot Wheels|2023 Hot Wheels line]].

Since 2013, Mattel has incorporated Treasure Hunts into other series instead of being their own series.

Treasure Hunts are distinguished by the 'circle flame' symbol. [[File:RTH.png|30px]]

==2023 Super Treasure Hunts==

In 2023, Mattel released a series of Super Treasure Hunts, hidden among the other series.

They are distinguished by one or more of the following: Spectraflame paint, Real Riders, extensive detail and a [[File:STH.png|40px]] symbol.

A new feature that was added in 2015 was a flame within a circle behind the vehicle on the [[Card|card]] like the Treasure Hunt logo, but gold colored.
