This is a list of [[Treasure Hunts]] from 2025.

==Treasure Hunts==
The 2025 Treasure Hunts Series is part of the [[List of 2025 Hot Wheels|2025 Hot Wheels line]].

Since 2013, Mattel has incorporated Treasure Hunts into other series instead of being their own series.

Treasure Hunts are distinguished by the 'circle flame' symbol. [[File:RTH.png|30px]]

==Super Treasure Hunts==
In 2025, Mattel released a series of Super Treasure Hunts, hidden among the other series.

They are distinguished by one or more of the following: Spectraflame paint, Real Riders, extensive detail and a [[File:STH.png|30px]] symbol.

A new feature that was added in 2015 was a flame within a circle behind the vehicle on the card like the one below, except it is the color gold.
