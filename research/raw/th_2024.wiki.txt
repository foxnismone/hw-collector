==2024 Treasure Hunts==
The 2024 Treasure Hunts Series is part of the [[List of 2024 Hot Wheels|2024 Hot Wheels line]].

Since 2013, Mattel has incorporated Treasure Hunts into other series instead of being their own series.

Treasure Hunts are distinguished by the 'circle flame' symbol. [[File:RTH.png|30px]]

== 2024 Super Treasure Hunts ==
In 2024, Mattel released a series of Super Treasure Hunts, hidden among the other series.

They are distinguished by one or more of the following: Spectraflame paint, Real Riders, extensive detail and a [[File:STH.png|40px]] symbol.

A new feature that was added in 2015 was a flame within a circle behind the vehicle on the [[Card|card]] like the one below, except it is the color gold.

==Master Set==
The Master Set was designed to look like a mechanics toolbox with removable drawers.  Each drawer can be pulled out and turned up for display once the bottom piece has been pulled open.  2,500 were produced.  Toy #HWX38.
